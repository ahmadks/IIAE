\begin{abstract}
In this paper, we introduce the Invariant Data Integrity Chain of Custody (IDICOC), a formal verification framework designed to quantify behavioral dissonance and entropy accumulation in complex dynamical systems. Unlike traditional computational models that attempt to enforce system-wide convergence, IDICOC functions as a structural auditor and isolation protocol. Through a coinductive gating mechanism, the framework maps data trajectories as semantically isolated objects, confining non-conforming states within a geometric sandbox to prevent interference with the system's identity constant. By establishing invariance as a pre-emptive structural constraint—where data incompatibility is identified through rigorous state-space drift monitoring—IDICOC acts as a system of structural immunity. We formalize the foundational measure-theoretic endofunctors, develop the explicit analytical formulations for the Behavioral Dissonance Metric ($D_s$) via Kantorovich-lifted pseudometrics, and operationalize these bounds through the \textit{Motor de Auditoría de Integridad Invariante} (MAII). Finally, we demonstrate this functionality using the Collatz trajectory as a benchmark, establishing a formal methodology for state-space preservation and entropy monitoring independent of the system's inherent ergodic or chaotic nature.
\end{abstract}
